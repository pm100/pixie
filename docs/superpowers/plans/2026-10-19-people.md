# People (faces) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Find faces in the library in the background, group them into people, let the user name / merge / hide people, and filter Browse by person.

**Architecture:** A new background pass, separate from the CLIP pass, runs YuNet (detector) and SFace (128-d recogniser) through ONNX Runtime. Each photo is scanned once; its faces are stored with normalised boxes and embeddings and assigned greedily to people by cosine similarity to a running centroid. A person filter joins the existing `search_hits` machinery (text search, tag filter), so the grid, viewer, map and When nav need no changes. A new `PeopleService` (models, worker, status event) sits beside `AnalysisService`; pause and retry act on both.

**Tech Stack:** Rust (`ort =2.0.0-rc.10`, `image`, `rusqlite`), Tauri 2 commands/events/protocol, Svelte 5 + TS.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-18-content-analysis-design.md` (People section). Rulings against the spec, taken here: (1) YuNet is MIT, not Apache (checked against the OpenCV Zoo LICENSE at the pinned commit; the spec is corrected in Task 10). (2) A `face_scans` table (one row per photo and faces version) is the queue marker, like `embeddings`, so photos with no faces are not rescanned. (3) The spec's background "tidy" merge is out of scope; manual merge is the remedy. (4) Face crops are cut on demand from the cached 1600 px viewer thumbnail (`pixie://face/{id}`), not stored. (5) Unnamed people with a single photo are not listed in the nav (noise); naming one, or a second photo joining it, lists it.

## Review Focus

1. A photo with no faces, an undecodable file, or a huge panorama: one scan row is recorded, the photo is not retried, the worker moves on (Task 6).
2. A detector or recogniser panic or error: the worker survives, three failures in a row back off and show an error, photos are not marked scanned (Task 6).
3. Tiny, blurry or low-score faces must not create or join people (Tasks 1, 6).
4. Merge, hide, and "not this person" keep counts, centroids and the nav consistent, and a person filter on a person that no longer exists or is hidden is cleared by the UI (Tasks 4, 9).
5. Rotated (EXIF) photos: boxes are fractions of the upright image, and the crop uses the upright viewer thumbnail (Tasks 6, 8).
6. A changed or replaced file loses its faces and scan row (Task 4).
